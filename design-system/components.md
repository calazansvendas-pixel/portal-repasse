# MORAR — Component Rules

## Brand direction
Use green + white as the signature combination. Photography should carry much of the emotional and commercial communication. Keep interfaces clean, warm, trustworthy and contemporary.

## Buttons
Primary: green background `#006B3F`, white text, 44–48px height, 8px radius.
Hover: `#004F32`.
Secondary: transparent/white background, green text and 1px green border.
Avoid pill-shaped buttons unless the product context specifically requires them.

## Cards
White surface, 14px radius, subtle border `#E5E8E6`, optional shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.06)`.
Large photography, concise copy, strong title and one clear CTA.

## Header
Prefer white header with green brand mark and restrained navigation. Primary commercial action may use a green CTA.

## Forms
Inputs: 48px height, 8px radius, 1px `#E5E8E6` border.
Focus: green border with a subtle green focus ring.
Do not overload forms with decoration.

## Images
Use `object-fit: cover`. Favor architecture, interiors, amenities, people and lifestyle photography.
Recommended ratios: 16:10 cards, 21:9 hero banners, 4:3 property imagery.

## Icons
Use a consistent outline icon family such as Lucide. Recommended stroke 1.75–2px.

## Responsive
Desktop: max content width 1200–1280px with 24px side padding.
Tablet: 2-column content grids.
Mobile: 1-column grids, simplified navigation and preserved CTA hierarchy.

## Don't
Avoid neon colors, dominant purple/blue palettes, heavy gradients, excessive glassmorphism, oversized shadows, futuristic fonts, excessive animation, and overly rounded generic SaaS styling.

## Important evidence note
The visual analysis is based on the Morar website and available public brand material. Exact CSS font-family and every individual color token were not reliably exposed by the textual inspection. The main green and the broader visual direction are grounded in the brand; extended scales, typography pairing and implementation tokens are recommendations for the app and should not be represented as official brand specifications unless validated against the site's source CSS/brand guidelines.
